This is a screening discussion guide, not a diagnosis. In an emergency, stop and call your local emergency number (India 112, UAE 998 for an ambulance). Do not enter real names, contact details or medical records.

    Before you start

    SwasthyaAI helps you understand which preventive screenings you could talk about with a health professional, and what to do next. It runs in guest mode. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere.

    What it does

    • Asks a few questions: age band, sex, tobacco use, family history.
    • Applies simple, visible rules to suggest topics to discuss.
    • Explains each topic in plain language with its source and limits.
    • Gives you a follow-up checklist and questions for your clinician.

    What it does not do

    • It does not diagnose, treat, triage emergencies or replace a clinician.
    • Its rules are demo placeholders. Local clinicians must approve them before any real use.
